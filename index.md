[Prosody](https://ukb-dt.github.io/too-tidy/) is not decoration; it is metabolic governance. Rhythm, cadence, pause, and emphasis determine when high-cost cognition is invoked and when it is bypassed. A well-formed prosodic structure carries meaning while suppressing prefrontal load, allowing coordination, memory, and action at a fraction of the energetic cost of explicit reasoning. This is why ritual, poetry, chant, slogans, and narrative persist where analytic clarity fails: prosody is a power-efficient control signal. Strip it away and truth may remain—but it becomes unaffordable.

## The [power-grid](https://ukb-dt.github.io/dt/) simile (literal, not poetic)

In an electrical grid:

* **Generation capacity is finite**
* **Demand fluctuates**
* **Peak loads are dangerous and expensive**
* **Baseload processes are cheap and continuous**
* **High-draw operations are scheduled, throttled, or deferred**

Uganda is a textbook case:

* Heavy industrial loads are pushed to **sleeping hours**
* Not because night is “better”
* But because **aggregate demand drops**, freeing capacity
* This prevents brownouts, grid instability, and equipment damage

No metaphor yet — just load management.

---

## Now map *only what is isomorphic*

### Grid ↔ Organism

* **Grid capacity** ↔ metabolic ceiling
* **Industrial machinery** ↔ PFC / System 2 / high-order control
* **Residential baseload** ↔ autonomic + habitual processes
* **Load shedding** ↔ fatigue, stress, cognitive shutdown
* **Night scheduling** ↔ ritual, prosody, habit, default cognition

The key point:

> The grid does not care about *truth*, *meaning*, or *correctness*.
> It cares about **staying within safe operating limits**.

So does an organism.

---

## Why prosody fits *exactly* here

Prosody is not “content.”
Prosody is **load shaping**.

* Smooth cadence = reduced peak draw
* Rhythm = predictability = easier regulation
* Repetition = cached control signals
* Emphasis = targeted high-power bursts
* Silence / pause = recovery window

This is identical to:

* staggering industrial startups
* smoothing demand curves
* avoiding simultaneous high-draw events

Prosody is **demand response**, not persuasion.
